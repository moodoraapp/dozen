How it works

Plan the week in about a minute

Answer a few questions once, then plan a new week whenever you’re ready.

  1. 1

    Tell Dozen about your week

    How many people you cook for, how many dinners you need, your weekly budget, your ZIP code and your store.

  2. 2

    See your dinners and the total

    Dinners with photos and cook times, and an estimated grocery total for your store. You see all of it before you subscribe.

  3. 3

    Shop one list

    Priced by the pack and grouped by aisle. Check items off as you shop, or add the list to Apple Reminders to shop together.

Priced by the pack

Stores sell eggs by the dozen, not one at a time.

A recipe needs half an onion; the store sells you a whole one. Dozen builds your list from real pack sizes and picks dinners that share them, so the rest of the spinach goes into Thursday’s pasta instead of the trash.

  • Every item on the list shows which dinners use it
  • Each dinner shows what it shares with the rest of your week
  • Whatever is left goes into next week’s plan first
  • Prices are adjusted for your region and your store

Questions

Good to know

How are grocery prices estimated?

We start from public average prices (U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics and weekly USDA retail reports) and our own checks of pack sizes, then adjust for your region and store. Prices are estimates and can differ from your store’s shelf price. If one looks off, press and hold the item on your list and tell us what you paid.

Which stores can I pick?

Walmart, Aldi, Lidl, H-E-B, Target, Safeway, Meijer, Kroger, Publix, Stop & Shop, Trader Joe’s, Albertsons, Whole Foods, Costco and BJ’s. If yours isn’t listed, pick Other store and Dozen uses average prices for your region. Dozen isn’t affiliated with any of these stores.

Do I need an account?

No. There’s no sign-in. Your subscription belongs to your Apple Account, and Restore brings it back on a new phone. If iCloud is on, your plan, favorites and pantry come back too.

Can Dozen handle food allergies?

Diet settings and foods you won’t eat help choose recipes, but they aren’t an allergen check. Always read ingredient labels.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Dozen. Deleting the app doesn’t cancel a subscription. If you cancel during the free trial, you won’t be charged.
