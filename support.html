Help and support

Dozen plans a week of dinners for your household and budget, then builds a grocery list priced by the pack. Dinners share the packs you buy, so less goes to waste.

Contact us

Email moodora.app@gmail.com. We usually reply within two business days. If something in a recipe or a price looks wrong, you can also report it right in the app.

Common questions

How are grocery prices estimated?

We start from public average prices (U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics and weekly USDA retail reports) and our own checks of pack sizes, then adjust for your region and store. Prices are estimates and can differ from your store’s shelf price. Dozen isn’t affiliated with any grocery store.

A price looks wrong. What can I do?

On the grocery list, press and hold the item and tell us what you paid. We use these reports to correct prices for everyone.

Something in a recipe is wrong.

Open the recipe and tap “Something wrong in this recipe?” at the bottom. Tell us what’s off; a note is optional.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Dozen. Deleting the app doesn’t cancel a subscription. If you cancel during the free trial, you won’t be charged.

How do I restore a purchase on a new phone?

Use the same Apple Account, open Dozen and tap Restore on the subscription screen. If iCloud is on, your plan, favorites and pantry come back too.

Can Dozen handle food allergies?

Diet settings and “foods you won’t eat” help choose recipes, but they aren’t an allergen check. Always read ingredient labels.

How do I delete my data?

Dozen has no accounts, and your plan isn’t stored on our servers. In the app, go to Profile and tap Erase all data. This deletes your plan, list, favorites, pantry and answers from your phone and from iCloud. If you use Dozen on another device, do the same there. Your subscription isn’t affected.
